About Me

I am a junior data analyst with a background that spans healthcare data systems, supply chain analytics, and revenue intelligence. My experience was built inside organisations where data accuracy had real consequences — medicine availability at Central Medical Stores, district health reporting for the Ministry of Health — not just dashboards for their own sake.

Alongside my operational experience, I have built a portfolio of end-to-end analytical projects: a live Streamlit forecasting app using Holt-Winters exponential smoothing, a global water crisis risk model across 100+ countries, and an RFM customer segmentation engine on 500K+ e-commerce records. These are projects I built because I find the problems genuinely interesting.

I hold a BSc (Hons) in Computing specialising in Business Intelligence & Data Analytics from the University of Sunderland, and I am a Cisco Certified AI Business Practitioner (AIBIZ) — reflecting my commitment to understanding how AI applies at the organisational strategy level, not just the technical one.
